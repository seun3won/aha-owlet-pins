The Back-and-Forth That Builds a Baby's Brain

Your baby makes a sound or a face. You answer. They answer back. Researchers keep returning to this ordinary back-and-forth as one of the things that matters most in early development.

The useful part is what it does not require. No app, no schedule, no equipment, no set time of day. It happens in the pauses you already have — nappy changes, the walk to the car, the minute before a nap.

This is general information, not medical advice.

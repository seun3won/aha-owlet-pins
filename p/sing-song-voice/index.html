Why You Use a Sing-Song Voice With a Baby

Almost everyone does it without deciding to. The pitch goes up, the vowels stretch, the sentences get shorter.

Infants show a preference for it in studies — and they were showing it long before anyone thought to tell parents this was a technique. It is one of the rare bits of parenting advice that mostly consists of permission to keep doing what you already do.

General information, not medical advice.

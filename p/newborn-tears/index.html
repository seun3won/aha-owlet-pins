When Do Babies Start Crying Real Tears?

Newborns cry hard and dry. It surprises a lot of first-time parents, because the crying is clearly real and the tears are clearly missing.

Tear glands are working from birth, but only at the volume needed to keep the eye moist. It takes weeks for them to produce enough to spill over. Most babies shed visible tears somewhere between two weeks and three months.

Ranges vary widely between healthy babies. If you have concerns about your baby's eyes, ask your pediatrician.

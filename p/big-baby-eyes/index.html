Why Big Baby Eyes Make You Want to Pick Them Up

Large eyes, round cheeks, a small chin, a big head relative to the body. That specific set of features reliably pulls a response out of adults.

It is measurable, and it is not limited to your own child — it is why puppies and cartoon characters are drawn the way they are.

General information, not medical advice.

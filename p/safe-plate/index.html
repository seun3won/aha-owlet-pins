Picky Eating Is a Repetition Problem, Not a Willpower Problem

Most picky-eating advice tells you to relax. This template counts four things instead: how many times a food has been offered, the highest stage it reached (see, touch, smell, lick, bite), whether a safe food was on the plate, and what got said at the table.

Each meal row then prints one line for tomorrow. Add a safe food. Praise less. Say nothing about the food. Serve it again. You are not deciding at dinner with a child pushing the plate away.

Inside: a food list with an exposure counter and progress bar, a meal log, and a weekly review with a verdict. Fourteen filled-in example meals show what two finished weeks look like. Duplicate it into Notion and it opens ready to use.

General information for organising your own family's meals. Not medical advice. If your child is losing weight, often gags or chokes at meals, or eats fewer than twenty foods, talk to your pediatrician or a feeding therapist.

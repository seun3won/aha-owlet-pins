Why Do Newborns Stare at Black and White?

Colour vision develops gradually over the first months. Early on, a newborn's visual system is far better at edges and contrast than at subtle differences in hue.

High-contrast black and white patterns are simply the easiest thing available for that system to lock onto. It is less about preference than about what is legible.

General information, not medical advice.

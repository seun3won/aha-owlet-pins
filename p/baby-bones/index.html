Why Does a Baby Have More Bones Than You?

Babies start with around three hundred bones. Adults end up with two hundred and six. Nothing goes missing — many of them fuse together as a child grows.

A lot of what starts out as cartilage hardens and joins over years. The skull is the version most parents meet first, because the soft spots are the gaps that have not closed yet.

General information, not medical advice.

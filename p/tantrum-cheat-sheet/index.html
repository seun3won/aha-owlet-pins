Toddler Tantrum Cheat Sheet (Free Printable)

A tantrum is the moment you have the least room to think. That is the whole problem with advice that arrives as a paragraph — you cannot read a paragraph while a two-year-old is on the floor of a supermarket.

So this is one page. What to say in the moment, when naming the feeling helps, and what to skip. It is meant to be printed and stuck somewhere you will actually see it, not saved to a folder you will never open again.

Every child is different, and none of this replaces advice from your pediatrician.

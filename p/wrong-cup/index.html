Why Does Your Baby Look in the Wrong Cup?

Hide a toy under one cup. Your baby finds it. Move it to the other cup while they watch — and they reach for the first cup again.

Piaget documented this in eight to twelve month olds. Later research adds other explanations, including immature inhibition and working memory limits, so nobody treats it as one settled cause. The observation itself holds: it is a stage, not a mistake.

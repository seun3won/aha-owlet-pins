Why Unfinished Things Stay Stuck in Your Head

Interrupted tasks tend to be remembered better than completed ones. The open loop keeps its own kind of attention.

It is a useful thing to know at nine at night, when the reason you cannot settle is not the day you had but the three things you did not finish in it. Writing them down is not a productivity trick so much as a way of closing the loop cheaply.

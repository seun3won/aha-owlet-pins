This calculator does simple arithmetic on published rules of thumb. It is general information, not medical or feeding advice, and it does not apply to premature babies, babies with medical conditions, or special formulas. Always make up formula exactly as the label says. Talk to your pediatrician or health visitor about how much your baby should drink.

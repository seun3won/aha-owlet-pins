This is a place to write things down, not a medical record. Feeding amounts, nappy counts and sleep vary widely between healthy babies, and this page does not tell you what is normal for yours. If you are worried about feeding, weight gain, or wet nappies, talk to your pediatrician or midwife rather than to a log. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere — it stays on this device.

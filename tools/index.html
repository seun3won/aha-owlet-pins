Free tools for parents

Small, single-purpose, and free. No signup, no app to install, nothing sent to a server.

Wake Window Calculator

Enter your baby's age and last wake-up time to see the typical window before the next nap.

Feeding & Diaper Log

Track feeds, nappies and naps for one day. Fill it in on your phone, or print a blank sheet.

Milestone Checklist

The CDC list of what most children do by each age, 2 months to 3 years. Tick and print for the checkup.

Formula Calculator

Weight in, AAP and NHS rule-of-thumb ranges out — per day and per bottle, in oz and ml.

Tantrum First-Aid

Pick the situation and get one calm sentence to say and three things to do, with a tantrum timer.

Screen Time Planner

Plan today's screen slots next to AAP and WHO guidance for your child's age. Print a weekly plan.

Adjusted Age Calculator

Born early? Work out corrected age from birth date and weeks of pregnancy at birth.

Baby Teeth Chart

Which teeth usually come in around your baby's age, with a place to log each one. Printable.

100 First Foods Checklist

Tick off first foods by group as your baby tries them. Printable for the fridge.

Potty Training Chart

A printable sticker chart with your child's name, plus a quick daily tally on your phone.

Routine Chart Maker

Morning or bedtime steps in pictures, start times worked back from your deadline. Printable.

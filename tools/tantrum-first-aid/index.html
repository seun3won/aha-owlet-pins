    When to check in with your child's doctor

    Cleveland Clinic lists these among reasons to contact a healthcare provider about tantrums:

    • tantrums keep going or get worse after age 4
    • tantrums often last longer than 15 minutes, or happen many times a day
    • your child hurts themselves or others, or breaks things
    • your child holds their breath, especially if they faint
    • you feel unsure how to keep everyone safe

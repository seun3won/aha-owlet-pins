Where the numbers come from
  • American Dental Association (MouthHealthy) — Primary Tooth Development chart (eruption ranges used here)

Eruption ranges are typical ranges published by the American Dental Association; many healthy children get teeth earlier or later, and in a different order. This page does not assess your child. It is general information, not medical or dental advice — ask your pediatrician or dentist about your child's teeth and teething.
